# Bill of materials: TPS5430 12 V to 5 V industrial buck converter

Stage 3 (part selection), updated 2026-11-06 by change bom-datasheet-verification. One row per refdes. Every fitted part was checked against its manufacturer datasheet on 2026-11-06 (research/parts/datasheet-verification.md). Figures marked VERIFIED cite the datasheet revision and page from that note. Figures read only from a typical curve are labelled as such, because they are not guaranteed limits. Live stock and lifecycle are not checked (SPEC §7).
Symbols are installed and pin-confirmed with symbol_pins; footprints are installed and confirmed with check_footprints. The symbol lib_id for each row is given in its Rationale.

## Intentionally absent
- **R_en (EN pull-up): not fitted, on purpose.** The TPS5430 internal ENA pull-up gives default-on. A pull-up to VIN would exceed the ~7 V ENA abs max (`protection.ena_pin_max_V`). It is not an omission.
- **Power-good output:** none, because the TPS5430 has no PG pin (SPEC §8).
